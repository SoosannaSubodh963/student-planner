About

A planner designed around real student life

Student Task Planner is a complete front-end web application built with HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. All information lives in the browser's LocalStorage, so your records survive page refreshes, logouts and restarts.

It ships with two separate experiences: a Student portal for personal planning and an Admin console for managing students, monitoring tasks and publishing targeted announcements.

  • Role-based access: students and admins see only their own area
  • Private records: every item is tied to its owner
  • Responsive design with light and dark themes
  • Reports with charts and CSV export for administrators
